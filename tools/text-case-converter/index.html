Why use a case converter?

Retyping text to fix its capitalization is slow and error-prone. This tool instantly reformats any block of text — a title you want in Title Case, an accidentally-caps-locked paragraph, or a heading you need in Sentence case — without you having to type it again.

How the word counter works

The counter updates live as you type or paste, and also after each case conversion. Word count splits on whitespace, character counts include and exclude spaces, and sentence count is an approximate count based on sentence-ending punctuation (., !, ?).
